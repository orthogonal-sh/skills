# Journal To Post

Use this skill when converting raw notes or reflections into public-safe social posts, essays, or updates.

## Workflow

1. Identify private details, names, client context, and sensitive facts.
2. Extract the general lesson, tension, or observation.
3. Rewrite in the desired voice and platform format.
4. Remove specifics that reveal private work or relationships.
5. Provide variants and note any remaining privacy risks.

## Privacy

- Default to abstraction.
- Do not publish or schedule without explicit approval.
