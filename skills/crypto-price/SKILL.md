# Crypto Price

Use when the user asks for current crypto prices, token comparisons, market moves, or a quick coin snapshot.

## Workflow

- Identify the token symbol, chain, quote currency, and time window.
- Fetch current price and relevant market context from a current source.
- Include timestamp, data provider, and quote currency.
- Avoid investment advice unless explicitly requested, and label uncertainty clearly.
