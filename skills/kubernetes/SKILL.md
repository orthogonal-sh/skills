# Kubernetes

Use this skill when a task involves Kubernetes clusters, kubectl, manifests, Helm, Kustomize, services, workloads, logs, or rollout health.

## Workflow

- Determine the cluster, namespace, context, workload, and whether the task is diagnostic or operational.
- Inspect resources, events, pods, logs, probes, images, config, secrets references, and rollout history before changing anything.
- Prefer declarative manifests and reversible rollouts; flag disruption, quota, permission, and production-impact risks.
- Verify with rollout status, pod health, service reachability, and relevant logs or metrics.
