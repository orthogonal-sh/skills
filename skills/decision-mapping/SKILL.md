# Decision Mapping

Use this skill when a technical, product, or operational decision needs structure.

## Workflow

1. State the decision, constraints, stakeholders, deadline, and success criteria.
2. List realistic options, including the option to defer or do nothing when valid.
3. Compare tradeoffs, reversibility, cost, risk, and evidence.
4. Recommend one path and record follow-up checks or decision triggers.
