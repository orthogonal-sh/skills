# Market News Analyst

Use this skill when a task involves financial market news, catalysts, company events, sector moves, or macro developments.

## Workflow

- Identify the security, sector, asset class, region, and relevant time window.
- Separate confirmed facts, market reaction, analyst interpretation, and speculation.
- Check timestamps, source quality, and whether the event is already priced in.
- Summarize implications with risks, open questions, and links to primary filings or official releases where possible.
