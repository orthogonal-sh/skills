# Smart Followups

Use when the user asks for follow-up ideas, next steps, or lightweight options after a response. Keep suggestions actionable and adapted to the current context.

## Guidelines

- Offer two to four options, not a long menu.
- Prefer concrete next actions over generic prompts.
- Do not add follow-ups after every answer; use only when it helps continuation.
- Avoid suggesting actions that would spend money or contact external parties without approval.
