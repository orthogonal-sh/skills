# LinkedIn

Use this skill when a task involves LinkedIn profiles, company pages, posts, jobs, hiring signals, or outreach research.

## Workflow

- Clarify the target person, company, role, location, timeframe, and whether the task is research or outreach preparation.
- Prefer public, user-authorized, or API-accessible data; respect platform limits and privacy boundaries.
- Extract only relevant professional context, and distinguish verified profile facts from inferred conclusions.
- Summarize findings with source context, confidence, and recommended next steps.
