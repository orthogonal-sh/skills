# Better Notion

Use this skill when a task involves improving Notion information architecture, databases, templates, pages, or workflows.

## Workflow

- Identify the workspace, page/database scope, users, properties, relations, and workflow pain points.
- Inspect existing structure before proposing schema or template changes.
- Favor simple properties, clear views, and maintainable conventions over elaborate setups.
- Verify with sample records, views, filters, permissions, and downstream automation impacts.
