# Network Scanner

Use this skill when inspecting a local or private network for devices, open ports, and exposed services.

## Capabilities

- Discover hosts and identify likely device types.
- Scan ports and summarize exposed services.
- Flag risky services, weak defaults, or unexpected listeners.
- Produce cautious remediation steps without disrupting the network.
