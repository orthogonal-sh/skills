# Stock Price Checker

Use this skill when the user needs quick price facts rather than a full investment memo.

## Workflow

1. Confirm ticker, exchange, asset type, and whether pre-market or after-hours data matters.
2. Fetch the latest price, prior close, day range, volume, and timestamp.
3. Calculate percent moves and simple comparisons when useful.
4. Mention market status and data delay.

## Notes

- Verify tickers before reporting.
- Use exact dates and times for historical prices.
