# telegram-offline-voice

Use this skill when handling Telegram voice notes or audio messages that need offline transcription, summarization, response drafting, or delivery review.

## Workflow

- Identify chat context, audio files, language, privacy constraints, and whether sending is requested.
- Transcribe locally when possible and mark inaudible sections or uncertain wording.
- Summarize the message, extract commitments or questions, and draft replies in the appropriate tone.
- Require explicit confirmation before sending messages or forwarding private audio.
