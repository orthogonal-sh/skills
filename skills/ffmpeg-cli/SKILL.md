# FFmpeg CLI

Use this skill when manipulating video or audio files with FFmpeg or ffprobe.

## Capabilities

- Convert formats, trim media, and extract clips.
- Inspect codecs, dimensions, duration, bitrate, and streams.
- Extract frames, thumbnails, waveforms, or audio tracks.
- Compress, transcode, normalize, and remux media assets.
