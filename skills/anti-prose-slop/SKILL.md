# Anti Prose Slop

Use this skill when revising text that feels generic, padded, evasive, or overly AI-like.

## Workflow

- Identify weak claims, stock phrases, hedging, throat-clearing, and repeated structure.
- Preserve the author's intent while making the prose more concrete.
- Cut filler before adding new language.
- Vary sentence shape and choose words that fit the audience.
- Keep revisions honest; do not invent specificity that the source cannot support.
