# US Stock Analysis

Use this skill when the user asks for analysis of NYSE, Nasdaq, AMEX, or OTC-traded US equities.

## Workflow

1. Verify the ticker, listing venue, market cap, sector, and reporting calendar.
2. Review SEC filings, earnings materials, analyst expectations, recent news, and price action.
3. Compare valuation and operating metrics against relevant peers.
4. Summarize thesis, catalysts, red flags, and what would change the view.

## Notes

- Include filing dates and reporting periods.
- Avoid personalized investment advice.
