# NotebookLM

Use this skill when a task involves NotebookLM, source-grounded notebooks, study guides, document Q&A, briefings, or citation-backed summaries.

## Workflow

- Identify the source set, notebook goal, audience, and required output format.
- Organize sources by topic, reliability, freshness, and gaps before generating summaries or questions.
- Keep claims grounded in the uploaded or referenced materials, and mark unsupported inferences clearly.
- Produce concise briefs, study guides, FAQs, timelines, or open-question lists as requested.
