# Cloudflare Email Service

Use this skill when setting up or debugging Cloudflare email workflows.

## Workflow

1. Identify the domain, DNS host, routing rules, Workers handlers, and desired mail flow.
2. Verify MX, SPF, DKIM, DMARC, forwarding, and catch-all behavior.
3. Keep secrets in Cloudflare bindings or environment-specific secret stores.
4. Test inbound and outbound paths with traceable sample messages.
