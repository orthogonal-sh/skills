# Crypto Gold Monitor

Use this skill when tracking Bitcoin, major crypto assets, gold, or related macro signals.

## Workflow

- Fetch fresh spot prices, percent changes, volume, and relevant macro context.
- Distinguish on-chain, exchange, ETF, futures, and macro data sources.
- Flag unusual moves, liquidity events, and scheduled catalysts.
- Keep summaries concise and timestamped.
- Avoid trading recommendations unless the user explicitly asks for analysis framing.
