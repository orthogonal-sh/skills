# Weather

Use this skill when weather could affect plans, travel, outdoor work, or a direct forecast question.

## Workflow

- Resolve the location and date range, including timezone when relevant.
- Use a current weather source or configured weather tool rather than stale memory.
- Highlight precipitation timing, temperature, wind, alerts, and uncertainty.
- Convert units to the user's locale unless asked otherwise.
- Keep routine forecasts short and actionable.
