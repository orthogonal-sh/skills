# Relationship Skills

Use when the user asks for help with communication, conflict, boundaries, repair attempts, or understanding another person's perspective.

## Workflow

- Separate observable facts, interpretations, feelings, needs, and requests.
- Draft language that is specific, kind, and boundaried.
- Consider timing, medium, and likely reactions.
- Avoid manipulation, surveillance, or pretending certainty about another person's intent.
