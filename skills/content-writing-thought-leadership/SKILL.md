# Content Writing Thought Leadership

Use this skill when writing B2B posts, essays, or campaign drafts meant to build authority.

## Stub

- Clarify audience, point of view, and desired action.
- Use specific examples instead of generic claims.
- Adapt length and format to the target platform.
