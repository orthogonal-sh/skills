# Content Writing Thought Leadership

Use this skill when the user wants essays, posts, speeches, op-eds, newsletters, or founder-style perspective pieces.

## Workflow

1. Clarify audience, thesis, channel, credibility basis, and desired level of polish.
2. Gather proof points, anecdotes, contrarian edges, and practical implications.
3. Draft with a strong argument, specific examples, and minimal filler.
4. Revise for voice, structure, evidence, and distribution format.

## Notes

- Avoid generic business language.
- Do not invent personal experiences or claims.
