# AI Music

Use this skill when a user wants to generate, edit, evaluate, or prompt for AI-created music or audio beds.

## Workflow

- Clarify intended use, mood, genre, duration, vocals, tempo, and licensing constraints.
- Write prompts with structure, instrumentation, energy arc, and negative constraints.
- Generate short candidates first, then iterate on the best direction.
- Check loopability, clipping, noise, abrupt transitions, and export format.
- Document provider, prompt, and usage constraints with the final file.
