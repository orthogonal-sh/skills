# Grocery List

Use this skill when planning meals, building shopping lists, or organizing pantry staples.

## Workflow

1. Gather dietary constraints, meals, servings, budget, and store preferences.
2. Convert meals into ingredients and quantities.
3. Deduplicate pantry items and group by store section.
4. Mark substitutions and optional items.
5. Save or export the final list in the requested format.

## Tips

- Keep staples separate from one-off ingredients.
- Ask before placing orders or changing shared lists.
