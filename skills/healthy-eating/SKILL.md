# healthy-eating

Use this skill when the user asks for meal planning, grocery ideas, nutrition tradeoffs, eating routines, or diet-compatible recipes.

## Workflow

- Gather goals, dietary restrictions, dislikes, budget, cooking tools, time, allergies, and household size.
- Suggest practical meals and substitutions with protein, fiber, calories, and prep effort in mind.
- Avoid medical claims; recommend professional guidance for clinical conditions or eating disorder concerns.
- Produce grocery lists, prep steps, and repeatable routines rather than fragile one-off plans.
