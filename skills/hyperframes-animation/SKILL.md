# Hyperframes Animation

Use this skill when creating or refining animated AI video scenes, transitions, product demos, explainers, or social clips.

## Workflow

- Define the visual goal, audience, duration, and delivery format.
- Break the animation into scenes with timing, camera moves, subject motion, and transitions.
- Keep text readable and avoid overloading frames with competing motion.
- Generate or render a small preview before committing to full output.
- Verify final dimensions, timing, legibility, and artifacts.
