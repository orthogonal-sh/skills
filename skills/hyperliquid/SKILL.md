# Hyperliquid

Use this skill for Hyperliquid market data, account review, funding analysis, or protocol research.

## Workflow

- Fetch current prices, funding, open interest, volume, and relevant account data when authorized.
- Distinguish spot, perpetual, vault, bridge, and protocol-level information.
- Check recent announcements or incidents for context.
- Summarize liquidation, leverage, and liquidity risks clearly.
- Do not execute trades or transfers without explicit approval.
