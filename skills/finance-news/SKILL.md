# Finance News

Use this skill when a task depends on current financial news or market-moving events.

## Workflow

- Clarify the asset, company, sector, geography, and freshness window.
- Check reputable financial news, filings, exchange notices, and primary company announcements.
- Distinguish confirmed events from rumors, commentary, or analyst opinion.
- Return a concise brief with event timing, market impact, source confidence, and follow-up watch points.
