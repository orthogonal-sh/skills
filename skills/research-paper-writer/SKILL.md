# Research Paper Writer

Use this skill when the user wants a formal academic-style paper or manuscript draft.

## When To Use

- Convert research notes into a paper outline or draft.
- Write abstracts, introductions, methods, results, related work, or limitations.
- Format content toward IEEE, ACM, or similar conventions.
- Improve scholarly tone without inventing citations.

## Workflow

1. Confirm venue or format expectations.
2. Gather source material and citation requirements.
3. Build an outline before drafting.
4. Separate claims from evidence.
5. Mark missing citations or uncertain claims explicitly.

## Guardrails

- Do not fabricate references, DOIs, results, or experiments.
- Keep limitations and threats to validity visible.
