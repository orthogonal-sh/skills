# meeting-prep

Use this skill before calls, sales meetings, investor conversations, interviews, vendor reviews, or internal planning sessions.

## Workflow

- Gather the meeting goal, attendees, company/project context, date, prior notes, and open threads.
- Build a concise brief covering background, likely priorities, risks, agenda, and useful questions.
- Include links or references to the source material used when available.
- End with expected decisions, desired outcomes, and follow-up capture fields.
