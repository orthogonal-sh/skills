# transcribee

Use this skill when audio or video content needs transcription, speaker separation, cleanup, summaries, quotes, or subtitle-ready exports.

## Workflow

- Identify source media, language, speaker count, desired accuracy, privacy constraints, and output format.
- Select local or remote transcription tooling based on confidentiality, duration, and quality needs.
- Clean filler only when requested, preserving meaning and marking uncertain words.
- Verify names, timestamps, speaker labels, and quoted excerpts before final delivery.
