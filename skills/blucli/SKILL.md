# blucli

Use this skill when a task involves Bluetooth device discovery, pairing, connection state, audio routing, battery checks, or troubleshooting local Bluetooth peripherals.

## Workflow

- Identify the target device, adapter, OS, and whether pairing or reconnecting is allowed.
- Inspect current adapter, scan, pairing, trust, connection, and battery state before changing anything.
- Avoid forgetting devices or changing audio routes unless explicitly requested.
- Verify the final connection, profile, route, and device status.
