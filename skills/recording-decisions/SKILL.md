# Recording Decisions

Use this skill when a decision should survive beyond the current conversation.

## Workflow

1. State the decision, date, owner, status, and scope.
2. Capture context, constraints, and why the decision is needed now.
3. List considered options and the reason each was accepted or rejected.
4. Document consequences, risks, dependencies, and reversal cost.
5. Add review triggers or dates when the decision may need revisiting.

## Checks

- Store the decision where the team expects ADRs or project notes.
- Keep private rationale out of public docs.
- Make uncertainty visible.
- Link to PRs, issues, design docs, and meeting notes.
