# Writing Postmortems

Use this skill when documenting an incident, outage, failed launch, or operational miss.

## Workflow

1. Gather impact, start and end times, affected users, alerts, and response notes.
2. Build a timeline from logs, messages, deploys, and monitoring data.
3. Separate contributing causes from symptoms and speculation.
4. Document what went well, what failed, and what should change.
5. Create concrete follow-up actions with owners and due dates.

## Checks

- Use blameless language while staying precise.
- Preserve exact times and timezones.
- Link evidence for important timeline entries.
- Avoid action items that are too vague to close.
