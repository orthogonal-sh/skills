# Pre Mortem Analyst

Use this skill when a plan should be examined before time or money is spent.

## Workflow

- Define the plan, assumptions, stakeholders, and success criteria.
- Imagine plausible failure scenarios across product, technical, operational, and market dimensions.
- Rank risks by likelihood, impact, reversibility, and detectability.
- Propose mitigations, checkpoints, and kill criteria.
- Summarize what must change before proceeding.
