# sonoscli

Use this skill when a task involves Sonos speaker control, room grouping, playback, queue changes, volume, favorites, alarms, or speaker diagnostics.

## Workflow

- Identify the target room or group and inspect current playback, grouping, and volume state.
- Prefer read-only status checks before issuing playback or grouping commands.
- Confirm before disruptive actions such as high volume, group-wide playback, or queue replacement.
- Verify the resulting room state, queue, source, and volume after changes.
