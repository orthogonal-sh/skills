# Miniflux News

Use this skill when reading or managing RSS feeds through Miniflux.

## Capabilities

- Review unread feeds and prioritize important articles.
- Summarize saved entries and extract source links.
- Monitor topics, products, companies, or people.
- Clean up noisy feeds and subscription lists.
