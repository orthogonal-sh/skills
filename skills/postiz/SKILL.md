# Postiz

Use when a user wants to draft, schedule, queue, or review social media posts through Postiz-supported channels.

## Safety Rules

- Ask before publishing, scheduling, or editing anything public.
- Confirm channel, account, copy, media, and scheduled time.
- Keep platform-specific formatting constraints in mind.
- Save drafts when approval is not explicit.
