# Stock Analysis

Use this skill for public-company research, watchlists, valuation notes, earnings prep, or market-moving news review.

## Workflow

- Pull current prices, filings, recent news, and key financial metrics from reliable sources.
- Separate reported facts from valuation assumptions and opinion.
- Note timeframe, currency, market hours, and data freshness.
- Compare peers when that improves the decision.
- Include risks, catalysts, and confidence level; do not present investment advice as certainty.
