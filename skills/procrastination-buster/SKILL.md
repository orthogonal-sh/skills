# Procrastination Buster

Use when a user says they are stuck, avoiding a task, overwhelmed, or unable to start.

## Guidelines

- Reduce the task to the next two-minute action.
- Remove ambiguity before adding motivation.
- Offer a short commitment window and a check-in point.
- Avoid shaming language or overplanning.
