# BBC News

Use this skill when the user asks for BBC coverage, UK-facing news context, or a comparison against other outlets.

## Workflow

1. Search BBC News for the topic, person, location, or event.
2. Check publication dates and whether newer updates supersede older reporting.
3. Summarize only the relevant facts and distinguish reporting from analysis.
4. Compare with other primary or local sources when the story is disputed or fast-moving.

## Notes

- Do not quote long article passages.
- Include dates for developing stories.
