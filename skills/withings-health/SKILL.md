# Withings Health

Use this skill when a task involves Withings devices, measurements, health trends, exports, account sync, or reports.

## Workflow

- Identify the metric, date range, device, account, and whether the request is informational or health-adjacent advice.
- Treat health data as sensitive and minimize exposure in outputs.
- Summarize trends carefully, separating measured data from interpretation.
- Verify units, timestamps, device source, and sync freshness before drawing conclusions.
