# Humanizer

Use this skill when a task involves making text sound more natural, direct, warm, concise, or human while keeping the original meaning.

## Workflow

- Identify the target audience, medium, desired tone, and facts that must remain unchanged.
- Remove filler, generic phrasing, and mechanical transitions without adding unsupported claims.
- Preserve names, numbers, commitments, and sensitive wording exactly where accuracy matters.
- Offer a polished version and, when useful, a shorter variant for faster review.
