# Research Company

Use this skill when asked to research a company, account, competitor, prospect, or potential partner.

## Workflow

1. Confirm the target company and research purpose.
2. Gather current website, product, team, funding, customer, market, and news data.
3. Separate verified facts from inference.
4. Build a concise brief with opportunities, risks, and relevant contacts.
5. Include sources and dates for material claims.

## Output

- Company snapshot.
- Product and market.
- Team and funding.
- Signals and risks.
- Recommended next action.
