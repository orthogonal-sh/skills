# News Aggregator Skill

Use this skill when the user wants a news digest, topic monitor, timeline, or cross-source summary.

## Workflow

1. Define topic scope, geography, sources, recency window, and alert threshold.
2. Collect stories from diverse outlets and primary sources.
3. Deduplicate wire pickups and cluster related developments.
4. Summarize what happened, what changed, what is uncertain, and what to watch next.

## Notes

- Clearly label breaking-news uncertainty.
- Avoid relying on a single outlet for contested claims.
