# Writing Runbooks

Use this skill when documenting repeatable operational procedures for incidents, maintenance, deployments, migrations, or service recovery.

Make the runbook executable under stress: list prerequisites, exact commands, expected outputs, rollback or mitigation paths, escalation criteria, and checks that prove the system is healthy again.
