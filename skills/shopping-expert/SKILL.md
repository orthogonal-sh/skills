# Shopping Expert

Discovery stub for a product research and shopping comparison skill.

Use when a user wants to choose a product, compare alternatives, check availability, or produce a short shopping recommendation with tradeoffs.

## Candidate Capabilities

- Search online and local product listings.
- Compare price, reviews, availability, and constraints.
- Summarize best picks with reasons.
- Track user preferences and avoid duplicate research.
