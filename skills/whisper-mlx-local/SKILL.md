# whisper-mlx-local

Use this skill when transcribing audio or video locally on Apple Silicon with MLX-based Whisper tooling or when privacy, offline use, or speed matters.

## Workflow

- Inspect source media, language, duration, channel layout, and desired output format.
- Choose model size and decoding settings based on quality, speed, and hardware constraints.
- Generate transcripts, timestamps, speaker or segment notes when available, and subtitles if requested.
- Check for hallucinated endings, repeated segments, language drift, and timecode alignment.
