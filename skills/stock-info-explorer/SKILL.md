# Stock Info Explorer

Use this skill when the user asks for a quick but grounded view of a public company, ticker, market move, or watchlist item.

## Workflow

1. Identify the ticker, exchange, company name, and the user's decision context.
2. Gather recent price action, market cap, fundamentals, filings, earnings dates, and relevant news from reliable finance sources.
3. Separate current facts from interpretation, and call out data freshness, market hours, and uncertainty.
4. Summarize catalysts, risks, comparable companies, and useful follow-up checks.

## Notes

- Verify symbols before analysis, especially for ADRs, dual listings, and similarly named companies.
- Do not present investment advice as certainty.
