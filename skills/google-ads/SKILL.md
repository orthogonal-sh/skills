# Google Ads

Use this skill when a task involves Google Ads campaigns, keywords, budgets, or reporting.

## When To Use

- Pull campaign, ad group, keyword, or search-term reports.
- Audit budgets, spend, conversions, and quality signals.
- Draft optimization recommendations.
- Prepare bulk changes for review.

## Workflow

1. Confirm account and date range.
2. Query performance data.
3. Segment by campaign, ad group, keyword, and device when useful.
4. Separate observations from recommended changes.

## Safety

Confirm before applying bid, budget, targeting, or creative changes.
