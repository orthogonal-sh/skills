# weekly-synthesis

Use this skill when summarizing a week of work, journals, meeting notes, chats, task logs, or repository activity into a durable review.

## Workflow

- Gather the week boundary, relevant projects, source files, transcripts, commits, and task lists.
- Separate events, decisions, shipped work, open loops, blockers, and lessons learned.
- Collapse repeated details into themes while preserving concrete dates, names, and links.
- Produce a short executive summary plus actionable follow-ups and memory-worthy notes.
