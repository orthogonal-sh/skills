# De-AI-ify

Use this skill when text sounds generic, inflated, or obviously AI-written and needs a more natural voice.

## Workflow

- Preserve the original meaning, factual claims, and audience.
- Remove filler, stock phrases, inflated adjectives, and unnatural transitions.
- Use concrete language, shorter sentences, and a voice suited to the context.
- Return the revised text with minimal commentary unless asked for notes.
