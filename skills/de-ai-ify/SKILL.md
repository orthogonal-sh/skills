# De-AI-ify

Use this skill when a task involves cleaning up text that sounds generic, over-polished, repetitive, or recognizably AI-generated.

## Workflow

- Identify the intended reader, channel, and level of formality.
- Replace vague claims with concrete wording already supported by the source material.
- Cut canned transitions, inflated adjectives, and repetitive structure.
- Preserve factual content, constraints, and author voice instead of inventing a new persona.
