# RingBot

Use this skill when the user asks an agent to make an outbound phone call.

## When To Use

- Call a business to ask hours, availability, pricing, or policies.
- Schedule or confirm appointments.
- Place simple orders by phone.
- Collect information that is not available online.

## Required Confirmation

Before calling, confirm:

- Phone number
- Caller identity
- Purpose of the call
- Script or key questions
- What information may be shared

## Call Plan

Prepare:

- Opening line
- Questions in priority order
- Acceptable outcomes
- Callback or voicemail instructions
- Summary format

## Safety

Never make calls that impersonate someone, pressure recipients, handle emergencies, or disclose sensitive information without explicit permission.
