# High End Visual Design

Use this skill when a user asks for a premium, editorial, launch-quality, or visually refined interface.

## Workflow

- Define what should feel premium for this specific domain.
- Use strong imagery or product visuals when the subject requires inspection.
- Keep typography, spacing, and composition disciplined.
- Avoid generic gradients, decorative blobs, and stock-like filler.
- Verify the final screen at realistic viewport sizes.
