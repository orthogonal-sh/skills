# Apple Photos

Use this skill when a task involves Apple Photos, photo libraries, albums, media exports, duplicate cleanup, metadata, or local visual asset workflows.

## Workflow

- Identify the target library, albums, date ranges, people, locations, and whether the task is read-only or mutating.
- Prefer metadata-aware inspection and exports over brittle filesystem assumptions.
- Preserve originals and avoid destructive edits unless explicitly requested.
- Verify results with sample media checks, counts, and album/library state where available.
