# Polymarket

Use this skill for prediction-market checks, odds monitoring, market summaries, or resolution-risk analysis.

## Workflow

- Fetch current market price, spread, liquidity, volume, end date, and resolution source.
- Read the market rules before interpreting probabilities.
- Compare related markets and relevant news when useful.
- Call out thin liquidity, ambiguous wording, and manipulation risk.
- Avoid treating market odds as facts.
