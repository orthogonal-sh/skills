# Financial Market Analysis

Use this skill for deeper market analysis beyond a simple ticker quote.

## Workflow

1. Define the asset, thesis, horizon, and benchmark.
2. Gather current filings, news, market data, and sector context.
3. Compare valuation, growth, profitability, risks, and catalysts.
4. Build bull, base, and bear cases when appropriate.
5. Present a balanced view with explicit assumptions.

## Guardrails

- Include data dates.
- Avoid certainty theater.
- Do not treat market commentary as personalized financial advice.
