# Financial Market Analysis

Use this skill when a task asks why markets are moving or how market factors relate.

## Workflow

- Clarify the market, instruments, timeframe, and intended level of detail.
- Gather price moves, rates, macro data, positioning, earnings, policy, and cross-asset context.
- Separate short-term drivers from structural themes and identify contrary evidence.
- Present scenarios, risks, and watch indicators without personalized investment advice.
