# Marketing Mode

Use this skill when the user asks for marketing strategy, messaging, positioning, growth experiments, launch plans, or campaign critique.

## Workflow

1. Identify the audience, product, offer, market category, price point, and conversion goal.
2. Draft positioning around the user's actual differentiators, not generic benefits.
3. Recommend channels, copy angles, assets, tests, and measurement plans.
4. Keep tactics specific enough that the next action is obvious.

## Notes

- Ask for approval before sending or publishing externally.
- Prefer concise, testable campaign ideas over broad branding language.
