# MeetGeek

Use this skill when working with MeetGeek meeting recordings or transcripts.

## Capabilities

- Retrieve or process meeting transcripts and highlights.
- Summarize decisions, action items, owners, and deadlines.
- Prepare follow-up notes for teams or CRM systems.
- Compare meeting notes against agenda and prior context.
