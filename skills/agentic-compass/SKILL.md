# Agentic Compass

Use this skill when the agent needs to choose what to do next, especially after a completed task or ambiguous status check.

## Capabilities

- Identify proactive follow-up work, deferred cron candidates, and shipping actions.
- Record avoidance rules when a behavior pattern is unhelpful.
- Convert reflection into a concrete next step rather than a vague note.
