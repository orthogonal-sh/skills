# Remotion Video Toolkit

Use this skill when creating or modifying videos with Remotion and React.

## Workflow

- Define the video duration, aspect ratio, script, visual structure, and assets.
- Implement scenes, timing, transitions, captions, and data-driven visuals in Remotion.
- Render locally or through the requested deployment target.
- Verify output framing, audio sync, caption readability, and render errors.
