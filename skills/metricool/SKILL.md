# Metricool

Use this skill when planning, scheduling, or analyzing social content with Metricool.

## Capabilities

- Prepare posts and publishing calendars.
- Review social metrics, reach, engagement, and campaign performance.
- Generate reporting summaries for channels or brands.
- Track competitor and content performance where available.
