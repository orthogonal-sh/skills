# CamelCamelCamel Alerts

Use this skill when the user wants Amazon price tracking, deal monitoring, or CamelCamelCamel alert summaries.

## Workflow

1. Collect product URLs, ASINs, target prices, and preferred alert destination.
2. Configure or inspect CamelCamelCamel alerts and RSS feeds.
3. Summarize price drops with current price, target price, historical context, and product link.
4. Send or draft notifications only to the approved destination.

## Safety

- Do not purchase items.
- Avoid exposing private wishlist or alert feeds.
