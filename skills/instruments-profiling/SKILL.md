# Instruments Profiling

Use this skill when diagnosing macOS, iOS, or Swift app performance with Apple Instruments.

## Capabilities

- Choose profiling templates for CPU, allocation, leaks, hangs, and energy.
- Capture traces and identify hot paths or memory growth.
- Interpret call trees, time profiles, and allocation lifetimes.
- Turn profiling evidence into targeted fixes and verification steps.
