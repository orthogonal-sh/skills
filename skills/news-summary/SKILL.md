# News Summary

Use this skill when a user wants a concise, sourced understanding of a current event.

## Workflow

- Identify the event, geography, relevant actors, and required recency.
- Compare multiple reputable sources and prioritize primary reporting or official statements.
- Build a short timeline of what happened, what is confirmed, and what remains unclear.
- Avoid amplifying speculation; label disputed claims and source them carefully.
