# HN

Use this skill when a task involves Hacker News search, launch feedback, technical discussions, startup mentions, or comment analysis.

## Workflow

- Identify the query, domain, user, story, or timeframe, and whether the user wants discovery or analysis.
- Search stories and comments, preserving item URLs, dates, points, comment counts, and parent context.
- Separate factual links from community opinions, and highlight recurring technical or market objections.
- Summarize the strongest signals, representative comments, and caveats.
