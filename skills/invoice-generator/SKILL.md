# invoice-generator

Use this skill when creating, reviewing, or converting invoices from notes, timesheets, contracts, estimates, or recurring billing details.

## Workflow

- Gather seller, customer, invoice number, dates, currency, line items, rates, taxes, discounts, and payment terms.
- Calculate subtotals, tax, total, due dates, and any deposit or partial-payment state.
- Keep financial assumptions explicit and flag missing legal or tax fields.
- Produce the requested format, such as Markdown, HTML, CSV, PDF-ready content, or accounting-system import rows.
