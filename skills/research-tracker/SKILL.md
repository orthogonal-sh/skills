# Research Tracker

Use this skill when research spans multiple sources, sessions, or unresolved questions.

## Workflow

- State the research question and the decision it supports.
- Record sources with dates, provenance, and reliability notes.
- Separate facts, interpretations, assumptions, and unresolved gaps.
- Maintain a concise status of what is known and what remains to check.
- Produce a handoff that lets another agent resume without repeating work.
