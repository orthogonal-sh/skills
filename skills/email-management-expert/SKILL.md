# Email Management Expert

Use this skill when helping with inbox review, email follow-up, or mailbox organization.

## Workflow

- Clarify the mailbox, search scope, urgency, labels, and whether drafts or actions are requested.
- Search with precise filters and summarize only the content needed for the task.
- Draft replies in the user's style, but ask before sending or taking external actions.
- Track follow-ups, deadlines, attachments, and unresolved questions.
