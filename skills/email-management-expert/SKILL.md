# Email Management Expert

Use this skill when the user asks for inbox triage, email organization, drafting, follow-up tracking, or inbox-zero workflows.

## Workflow

1. Identify the mailbox, search criteria, and time sensitivity.
2. Classify messages by urgency, sender, project, and required action.
3. Draft replies separately from sending.
4. Archive, label, or snooze only when requested or clearly allowed.
5. Summarize actions and unresolved items.

## Safety

- Ask before sending email.
- Keep private message contents scoped to the task.
- Do not expose credentials or mailbox tokens.
