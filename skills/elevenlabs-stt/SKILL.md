# ElevenLabs STT

Use this skill when a user wants audio transcribed with ElevenLabs, including diarization, timestamps, language hints, or structured transcript output.

## Notes

- Confirm whether the audio may be sent to an external API.
- Keep source audio paths and transcript filenames paired.
- Use speaker labels and timestamps when the transcript will drive follow-up tasks.
