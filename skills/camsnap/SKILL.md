# camsnap

Use this skill when a task involves taking camera snapshots, inspecting video streams, validating camera availability, or comparing frames from local or network cameras.

## Workflow

- Identify the camera name, URL, credentials context, desired resolution, and snapshot purpose.
- Prefer still-image endpoints or short captures over long recordings when a snapshot is enough.
- Store outputs with timestamps and camera identifiers.
- Verify that the image is nonblank, current, and from the expected camera before using it.
