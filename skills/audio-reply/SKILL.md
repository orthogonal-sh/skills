# Audio Reply

Use this skill when a task involves creating a spoken response, voice note, audio message script, or TTS-ready reply.

## Workflow

- Identify the recipient, channel, desired tone, length, and whether the audio will be sent externally.
- Draft for speech: short sentences, natural pauses, and no dense formatting.
- Confirm before sending or publishing audio externally.
- Verify generated audio for pronunciation, pacing, and unintended artifacts before delivery.
