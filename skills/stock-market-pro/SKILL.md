# Stock Market Pro

Use this skill when the user asks for ticker lookup, market snapshots, fundamentals, or portfolio-adjacent analysis.

## Workflow

1. Resolve tickers, exchanges, asset classes, and date ranges.
2. Fetch current prices and relevant historical data.
3. Review fundamentals, catalysts, technical context, and comparable assets.
4. State uncertainty, data timestamp, and limitations.
5. Avoid personalized financial advice unless properly framed as general information.

## Output

- Use concrete numbers with dates.
- Explain what moved and why.
- Distinguish news, metrics, and interpretation.
