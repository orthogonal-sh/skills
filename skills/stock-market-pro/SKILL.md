# Stock Market Pro

Use this skill when a task asks for current or historical public-company market research.

## Workflow

- Identify the ticker, exchange, timeframe, and whether the user wants research, monitoring, or comparison.
- Gather current price context, fundamentals, filings, news, analyst signals, and sector benchmarks from reliable sources.
- Separate facts from interpretation, and flag stale, delayed, or incomplete data.
- Summarize catalysts, risks, valuation notes, and watch items without giving personalized financial advice.
