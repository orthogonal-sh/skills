# Crypto Tracker

Use this skill when monitoring crypto assets, wallets, protocols, or market conditions.

## Workflow

- Clarify the asset, chain, wallet, protocol, exchange, and timeframe.
- Gather prices, volume, liquidity, funding, on-chain activity, governance, and relevant news.
- Watch for contract, bridge, oracle, exchange, and regulatory risks.
- Label uncertainty and avoid personalized trading advice.
