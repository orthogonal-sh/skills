# icloud-findmy

Use this skill when helping with Find My device location context, lost-device workflows, account/device status, or iCloud location troubleshooting.

## Workflow

- Confirm the device, account context, authorization boundary, and whether the task is read-only or may trigger external actions.
- Treat location data as highly private and share only the minimum necessary details.
- Check freshness, battery, connectivity, device state, and likely reasons for stale or missing location.
- For lost-device actions such as sound, lock, erase, or message display, require explicit confirmation.
