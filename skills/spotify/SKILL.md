# Spotify

Use this skill when a task involves Spotify search, playlists, tracks, albums, artists, listening history, or playback controls.

## Workflow

- Identify whether the task is metadata lookup, playlist management, recommendation, or playback control.
- Search by stable IDs or URLs when available, and preserve track, artist, album, duration, and explicitness metadata.
- For playlist edits or playback actions, confirm scope and avoid surprising user-visible changes.
- Verify final playlist contents, saved state, or playback status as appropriate.
