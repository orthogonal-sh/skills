# General Video

Use this skill for video tasks that do not fit a narrower video skill.

## Workflow

- Clarify the purpose, audience, format, duration, and distribution channel.
- Choose the right path: storyboard, generation prompt, edit plan, render, or critique.
- Account for visuals, narration, captions, music, pacing, and accessibility.
- Keep deliverables concrete: scripts, shot lists, prompts, edit instructions, or QA notes.
- Check output constraints such as aspect ratio, file size, codec, and rights.
