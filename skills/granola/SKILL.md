# Granola

Use this skill when a user wants to find, summarize, or extract action items from Granola meeting notes and transcripts.

## Notes

- Treat meeting notes as private by default.
- Preserve speaker, date, and meeting title metadata when available.
- Extract decisions and follow-ups separately from general discussion.
