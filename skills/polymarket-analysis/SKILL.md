# Polymarket Analysis

Use this skill when analyzing Polymarket prediction markets without placing trades.

## Workflow

- Search or identify the market, outcomes, prices, liquidity, and time horizon.
- Compare implied probabilities against outside evidence and related markets.
- Check for arbitrage, whale movement, volume changes, sentiment, and momentum signals.
- Present uncertainty clearly and separate analysis from trade execution.
