# Stock Evaluator V3

Use this skill for structured public-equity evaluations where the user wants a buy/hold/sell-style research memo, scorecard, or comparison.

## Workflow

1. Confirm ticker, time horizon, risk tolerance, and whether the user wants long-only, short, or neutral analysis.
2. Review business model, segment performance, financial statements, valuation multiples, analyst expectations, ownership, and news.
3. Score growth, profitability, balance sheet, valuation, momentum, management quality, and downside risks.
4. Produce a concise thesis with bull/base/bear cases and clear uncertainty.

## Notes

- Cite fresh market data when available.
- Avoid guaranteeing returns or making personalized financial advice claims.
